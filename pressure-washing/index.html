Pressure Washing questions homeowners ask

Is pressure washing safe for vinyl siding?

Yes, when it's done right. Siding is usually cleaned with soft washing, which uses low pressure and cleaning solution instead of a high-pressure blast that could force water behind the panels.

How often should I pressure wash my house?

Once a year or every other year is common. Homes in shady spots may need it more often to keep green mildew from building up.

Can pressure washing damage my deck?

Too much pressure can splinter wood. Experienced pros use the right pressure and technique for wood and composite. Cleaning is also a good first step before sealing or staining.

What's soft washing?

Soft washing uses low pressure and a cleaning solution to remove mildew, algae, and black streaks from siding and roofs without damaging them.
