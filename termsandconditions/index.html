1. What we do

South Jersey Home Repairs is a referral network. We connect homeowners in South Jersey, mainly in Camden and Gloucester counties, with independent contractors.

  • We are not a contractor. We do not perform, supervise, manage, inspect, or guarantee any home improvement, repair, or other work. We do not sell home improvements, give estimates or quotes for work, or enter into contracts for work with homeowners.
  • Contractors are independent businesses. Contractors in our network are not our employees, agents, partners, or subcontractors. We do not control how, when, or at what price they do their work.
  • Contractors pay us, homeowners don't. Contractors pay us for referrals. Homeowners never pay us anything.

2. For homeowners

2.1 Free, with no obligation

Our service is free for homeowners. You are never obligated to hire a contractor we refer to you.

2.2 Your agreement is with the contractor

Any estimate, price, contract, deposit, payment, schedule, permit, warranty, or change to the work is solely between you and the contractor. We are not a party to any agreement between you and a contractor.

2.3 Problems with a contractor or their work

If you have any problem with a contractor or their work, including quality, delays, damage, cost, cleanup, warranty claims, or conduct, you must take it up directly with the contractor. South Jersey Home Repairs is not responsible for any contractor's work and does not resolve disputes between homeowners and contractors. Because we do not perform or charge for the work, we do not issue refunds for it.

If you let us know about a problem, we may, at our choice, offer to match you with a different contractor, but we are not required to.

2.4 Check the contractor before you hire

We make reasonable efforts to refer contractors we believe are reputable. We do not guarantee any contractor's licensing, registration, insurance, qualifications, availability, pricing, workmanship, or conduct, and that information can change at any time. Before you hire anyone, you should:

  • Verify the contractor's New Jersey Home Improvement Contractor registration, and any trade license the work requires, with the New Jersey Division of Consumer Affairs.
  • Ask for proof of insurance.
  • Get a written contract. New Jersey requires a written contract for home improvement work over $500.
  • Confirm who is responsible for any permits and inspections.
  • Avoid paying the full price before the work begins.

When you submit a request or contact us, you agree that we may contact you by phone, text, or email about your project. You also agree that we may share your name, contact information, address or zip code, and project details with one or more contractors in our network so they can contact you. We share your information only with contractors we refer you to. We do not sell or give it to anyone else, such as marketers or list brokers.

2.6 Accurate information

You agree that the information you give us is accurate, and that you own the property or are authorized to request work on it.

2.7 Timing and emergencies

Contractors usually reach out within 24 hours, but we cannot guarantee when, or whether, a contractor will contact you or be available. We are not an emergency service. For a gas leak, fire, flooding, or any situation that puts people or property in immediate danger, call 911 or your utility company first.

3. For contractors

3.1 Your business is your responsibility

You are an independent business. You are solely responsible for:

  • Holding a current New Jersey Home Improvement Contractor registration and any trade license your work requires.
  • Carrying the insurance required by law, including general liability insurance, and workers' compensation if you have employees.

  • Your pricing, estimates, warranties, workmanship, employees, and subcontractors.
  • Your taxes.

You agree to give us proof of your registration, licensing, and insurance when we ask, and to tell us right away if any of them lapse.

3.2 How referrals work

When we have a lead that fits your trade and service area, we will contact you to offer it. A lead is "qualified" when we have confirmed the homeowner's contact information is real and reachable, spoken with the homeowner about the scope of the job, and confirmed the property is in your service area and the job matches your trade.

We send each lead to one contractor, unless the homeowner asks us for more than one quote. If that happens, we will tell you before you accept the lead.

3.3 Accepting a referral, including verbal acceptance

You accept a referral when you tell us you want it, whether by phone, in person, by text, or by email. A verbal acceptance is just as binding as a written one.

By accepting a referral, you agree to pay the qualified lead fee for that lead and the referral percentage on any job that closes with that homeowner, as described in these Terms. We keep a record of each acceptance, including the date, the homeowner, and how you accepted, and those records are used to determine what you owe. If you don't want a lead, tell us before you accept it and you won't be charged.

3.4 Fees

Qualified lead fee. $50 per qualified lead for lower-ticket trades, and $75 per qualified lead for higher-ticket and technical trades. We will confirm which fee applies to your trade when you join. The lead fee is owed for each lead you accept, whether or not the job closes.

Referral percentage on closed jobs. When a referred job closes, you owe a percentage of the closed job value:

  • $500 to $1,999: 5%
  • $2,000 to $4,999: 4%
  • $5,000 to $14,999: 3%
  • $15,000 to $29,999: 2%
  • $30,000 and up: 1%

Jobs under $500 owe the lead fee only.

Lead fee credit. The lead fee you already paid is credited against the referral percentage on the same job. For example, a $6,000 job closes at the 3% tier, so $180 is owed. The $75 lead fee already paid is credited, leaving $105. If the referral percentage is less than the lead fee, nothing more is owed, and the lead fee is not refunded.

What "closed" means. A job is closed when the homeowner signs a contract, accepts your estimate, or pays a deposit, whichever happens first. The "closed job value" is the total price agreed between you and the homeowner for the work, not counting sales tax, including any change orders, add-ons, and additional work you agree to for that homeowner.

3.5 You close your own jobs

Once you accept a referral, you are responsible for contacting the homeowner, scheduling the appointment, giving the estimate, and closing the job. We do not sell, negotiate, or close jobs on your behalf, and you cannot rely on us to close a job for you. We are not responsible if a referral does not turn into work.

3.6 Reporting closed jobs to us

You must tell us in writing, by text or email, within 7 days after a referred job closes. Include the homeowner's name, a short description of the job, and the closed job value. You must also tell us in writing within 7 days of any change order or additional work that changes the closed job value. A phone call alone does not count. Follow it with a text or email so there is a record.

We also follow up with homeowners directly, and we may confirm with them whether a job closed and for how much. If we learn that a referred job closed and was not reported to us, we will invoice you for the referral percentage based on the best information we have, and we may remove you from our network.

3.7 Repeat and later work

A homeowner we refer to you remains our referral for 12 months from the date you accept the lead. The referral percentage applies to every job you close for that homeowner during those 12 months, including later jobs. You agree not to avoid fees by closing a job outside these Terms, for example by having the homeowner contract with a related business.

3.8 Invoices and payment

We send invoices electronically. Payment is due within 15 days of the invoice date, by credit or debit card or bank transfer (ACH), with no processing fees. If an invoice is not paid on time, we may pause or stop sending you referrals until your account is current. Unpaid balances may be sent to collections, and to the extent the law allows, you will be responsible for reasonable costs of collection, including attorney's fees.

3.9 Leads that turn out to be bad

If a lead you accepted turns out to be unreachable or invalid, for example a wrong number or a duplicate, tell us in writing within 7 days of accepting it. We will review it and may credit the lead fee.

3.10 No guarantee of volume

Joining our network does not give you an exclusive territory or trade, and we do not guarantee any number of leads or that any lead will turn into a job.
