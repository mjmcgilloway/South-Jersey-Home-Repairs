Roofing questions homeowners ask

How do I know if I need a roof repair or a full replacement?

A few missing shingles or one leak around a vent or chimney can usually be repaired. If the roof is 20 or more years old, has curling or bald shingles, or leaks in more than one spot, replacement is often the better value. A roofer can tell you which after an inspection.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most asphalt shingle replacements on a typical South Jersey home take one to three days, depending on the size, the pitch, and the weather.

Will homeowners insurance pay for my roof?

Insurance usually covers sudden damage, like shingles torn off in a storm or a tree falling on the roof. It usually doesn't cover a roof that's simply worn out. Take photos of any storm damage and contact your insurer early.

Can I replace just part of my roof?

Sometimes. A damaged section can often be repaired, but matching older shingles is hard, and a partial replacement on an aging roof may not last as long as the rest of the work. Your roofer can walk you through the trade-offs.
